Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MS License Manager ("we", "our", or "us") handles information when you use our application and services. We prioritize data privacy and data minimization.

1. Information We Collect

We collect minimal information necessary to provide authentication and license management services:

  • Account Information: Primary email address provided during Google Identity Sign-In to verify user identity and license eligibility.
  • License Data: Subscription tier, plan category, duration, and expiration timestamps associated with the account.

2. How We Use Information

The collected information is used strictly for:

  • Authenticating administrative access and verifying user authorization.
  • Managing application licenses, plan assignments, and activation status.

3. Data Protection & Security

We employ industry-standard security measures, including Google Authentication OAuth protocols and encrypted database connections, to ensure user data remains protected against unauthorized access.

4. Information Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share user personal information or license records with third parties.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

User records and associated license data are retained only as long as required to fulfill service management functions. Users may request account record removal by contacting official support.
